Hand Window

Stretch a window between your hands. Inside it, you are a drawn character.

  • Pinch both handsa thin band opens between them
  • Open and spreadthe band widens, then takes over the frame
  • Cover the lensuncover to land on the next look
  • Pinch againback to the band; drop your hands to close it
