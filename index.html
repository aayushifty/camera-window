Hand Window

Stretch a window between your hands. Inside it, the world turns anime, comic or thermal, your face becomes a hero mask, or your eyes take on a new power. Friends can frame their own window at the same time.

  • Pinch both handsa thin band opens between them
  • Open and spreadthe band widens, then takes over the frame
  • Cover the lensuncover to land on the next look
  • Pinch againback to the band; drop your hands to close it
